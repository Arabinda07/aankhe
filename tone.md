# Parichay Voice & Tone Guide

This document defines the language, voice, and tone for all Parichay copy—from meta tags to UI microcopy to landing pages.

## The Core Identity

Parichay is a "Smoked Lac & Sandal Paper" studio. It feels like a sealed letter, high-end stationery, and quiet intimacy. It is fiercely anti-surveillance, but we communicate that boundary with calm confidence, not militant tech-bro anger.

Our users are Millennials and Gen-Z professionals who want to share their authentic selves and set boundaries at work or in life. But they are completely exhausted by apps that hoard their data to do it.

## The Voice

**Inviting, boundary-focused, and unmistakably human.**

1. **Relatable & Direct:** Speak to the real problem (e.g., "messy drafts", "skip the misunderstandings"). Don't hide behind corporate jargon.
2. **Technical, but not dry:** We are a 100% local, client-side app with zero database. Frame this technical fact as an emotional benefit: "Nobody is watching," "Your messy drafts stay yours."
3. **Calm & Unhurried:** We don't use aggressive marketing (e.g., "Supercharge your productivity!"). We use quiet confidence ("Your story, always ready").

## The "Unslop" Rules (What to Avoid)

Never use AI-generated filler. If it sounds like a generic B2B SaaS dashboard or a botanical wellness app, rewrite it.

**Kill these words:**
*Delve, tapestry, testament, seamless, robust, leverage, cutting-edge, holistic, paramount, pivotal.*

**Kill these patterns:**
- **The "SaaS Cheerleader":** "Great job! You've successfully updated your profile!" 
  *Instead use: "Profile updated."*
- **Hedging:** "It's important to note that..." 
  *Just make the point.*
- **Performative Empathy:** "We understand how hard it is to write about yourself." 
  *Instead use: "Writing about yourself is awkward. Start small."*
- **Corporate Fluff:** "Optimize your cross-functional alignment."
  *Instead use: "Help your team figure out how you work."*

## Copy Examples

**Meta Description (The Standard):**
> "Skip the misunderstandings. Build a personal operating manual to share how you actually work. It runs 100% locally in your browser—no servers, no accounts, so your messy drafts stay strictly yours."

**Empty State (UI):**
> *Bad:* "There is no data here yet. Click the button below to seamlessly start your journey!"
> *Good:* "Nothing here yet. Start writing when you're ready."

**Privacy Explanation:**
> *Bad:* "We leverage industry-leading local storage to ensure your data remains robustly protected."
> *Good:* "Parichay has no database. Everything you type stays in your browser until you choose to share it."
