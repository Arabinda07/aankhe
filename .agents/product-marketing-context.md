# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-05-14*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** A private, client-side studio for creating personal operating manuals.
**What it does:** Ankahe lets you write personal "manuals" explaining how you work, communicate, and operate. It runs entirely in the browser memory. You write the unedited truth, choose what to share, and generate a secure link containing only those selected answers.
**Product category:** Personal Manual / Communication Cheat Sheet
**Product type:** Client-side Web App
**Business model:** Free, no accounts, no server.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** N/A (Individuals)
**Decision-makers:** Individuals tired of repeating themselves to managers, partners, friends, or new teammates.
**Primary use case:** Creating a cheat sheet for a new relationship (work or personal) to avoid misunderstandings.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Bypass the exhaustion of explaining communication preferences.
- Set boundaries clearly without awkward conversations.
- Clarify misunderstandings before they happen.
**Use cases:**
- Onboarding with a new manager.
- Starting a new "situationship" or personal relationship.
- Syncing with a teammate on work styles.
- Having a difficult conversation.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** People are not mind readers. Explaining your communication style, stress responses, and boundaries repeatedly is exhausting and often leads to friction.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Traditional user manuals (Notion, Google Docs) are too public and hard to tailor.
- Standard notes apps lack a structured way to separate private thoughts from shared output.
**What it costs them:** Unnecessary friction, misread texts, crossed boundaries, and wasted emotional energy.
**Emotional tension:** The dread of a difficult conversation or the anxiety of being misunderstood by someone new.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- **Zero persistence:** No backend, no accounts, no database. Everything lives in the browser tab.
- **Granular visibility:** Users categorize each answer as Share, Private, or Hide. Only "Share" goes into the final URL.
- **Tactile privacy:** The URL itself is the database (compressed state in the hash).
**How we do it differently:** The application forces a local-first, ephemeral approach. When the tab closes, unshared data is gone forever.
**Why that's better:** It creates a truly safe space to write the "feral, unedited truth" first, before filtering it for an audience.
**Why customers choose us:** They want to share an operating manual without giving their data to a SaaS company.

## Brand Voice

**Style:** Editorial, concise. Short, bursty sentences.
**Personality:** A high-end stationery store. Quiet, intimate, emotionally precise. 

## Goals
**Business goal:** Provide a safe, beautiful utility for personal communication.
**Conversion action:** Generate a link.
