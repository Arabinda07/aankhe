# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-05-16*

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** A private, client-side intro page for the things people usually misread.
**What it does:** Parichay lets users answer guided questions about how they communicate, relate, work, and set boundaries. They choose whether each answer is Share, Private, or Hide, then review and send only the included version as a link, QR code, image, or PDF.
**Product category:** Private Intro Page / Relationship Cheat Sheet
**Product type:** Client-side web app
**Business model:** Free, no accounts, no server.

## Target Audience

**Target companies:** N/A.
**Decision-makers:** Individuals who are tired of being misunderstood by someone new.
**Priority audience:** Gen Z and Millennial users in new relationships, talking stages, close friendships, roommate dynamics, chosen-family dynamics, partnerships, new collaborations, or emotionally careful conversations.
**Secondary audiences:** New teammates, interns, managers, clients, partners, friends, co-parents, and people preparing for a hard talk.
**Primary use case:** Creating a short intro someone can actually read before they guess wrong.

## Jobs To Be Done

- Avoid being misread in texts, plans, boundaries, pace, tone, or conflict.
- Say sensitive context once instead of explaining the same pattern repeatedly.
- Share a version that feels safe enough to send and specific enough to help.
- Keep private thoughts local while still shaping a useful public version.
- Prepare for a new connection without making the conversation feel like therapy, HR, or a personality test.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** People are not mind readers. Relationships fail quietly when someone guesses wrong about tone, timing, boundaries, care, stress responses, or what support is supposed to look like.

**Why alternatives fall short:**

- Notes apps are private but unstructured.
- Notion or Google Docs can feel too public, too formal, or too much like homework.
- Personality tests over-label people and can sound like an explanation instead of context.
- Chat threads are immediate, messy, and easy to misread.

**What it costs users:** Awkward clarifications, defensive spirals, crossed boundaries, unread walls of text, and the feeling of being too much or not clear enough.

**Emotional tension:** "I want them to understand me, but I do not want to overexplain or expose everything."

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**

- **Client-side only:** No backend, account, database, server persistence, or analytics.
- **Granular visibility:** Every answer can be Share, Private, or Hide.
- **Share-safe links:** Only Share/included answers enter the URL.
- **Artifact payoff:** The user leaves with a composed intro page, not a raw form dump.

**How we do it differently:** Parichay treats private drafting and public sharing as separate states. Users can write honestly first, then decide what leaves the page.

**Why that's better:** It lets people prepare sensitive context without handing their data to a SaaS product or sending an unfiltered note.

## Current UI Notes

- The public navigation says "Create intro," "How it works," and "Privacy."
- The landing hero says "Your story always ready."
- The main CTA says "Create your intro."
- Setup asks who the intro is for, what they should understand, and how long it should be.
- Current recipient choices are Work, Someone close, Hard conversation, and Two of us.
- Artifact review is a three-step flow: Preview, Review sharing, Send / Export.
- Share / Private / Hide is the core privacy control and should stay plain.

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Honest, calm, private, non-judgmental, direct.
**Style:** Editorial, concise. Short, grounded sentences.
**Personality:** High-end stationery for emotionally precise context.

Do not write in generational slang. The Gen Z and Millennial fit comes from the job it does: making boundaries, care, pacing, and misreads easier to handle.

## Goals

**Business goal:** Make Parichay feel like a necessary utility for modern relationships and sensitive context sharing.
**Activation action:** Start an intro.
**Payoff action:** Review and send an included version.
**Trust goal:** Users understand that private answers stay local and hidden answers are omitted.
